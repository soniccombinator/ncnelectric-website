Michelle brings more than 20 years of experience in the electrical industry, with a background in residential electrical work, home building, and commercial electrical estimating. Originally from Virginia, she has made Florida home and brings a hands-on understanding of construction and electrical work to every project she estimates.

With experience ranging from residential construction to complex commercial projects, Michelle enjoys the challenge of putting all the pieces together, digging into the details, and making sure nothing gets overlooked. She believes a good estimate is about more than numbers—it’s about understanding the project, anticipating challenges, and setting the team up for success.
