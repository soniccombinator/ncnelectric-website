A U.S. Navy veteran and accomplished entrepreneur, Paul has led NCN Electric since 2020, guiding the company through strategic growth, modernization and cultural transformation. With a background in aviation and technology, he co-founded and grew several multimillion-dollar businesses before NCN. A graduate of the U.S. Naval Academy and the UCLA Anderson School of Management, Paul is known for visionary leadership, a commitment to continuous improvement, and turning challenges into opportunities.
